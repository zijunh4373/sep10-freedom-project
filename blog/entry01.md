## Choosing a Topic

The topic I decided on for my Freedom Project is **soccer**. I chose this topic because soccer is a sport that I enjoy and really love. I have always found the game interesting, from the players and teams to the strategies used during matches. I also wanted to see how technology has changed soccer despite knowing a great amount and how it might continue to improve the sport in the future.

Some examples of technology in soccer are Video Assistant Referee (VAR), goal-line technology, systems that help coaches analyze player performance, and even something as simple as a spray. I find these innovations interesting because they can affect important decisions during matches and help teams understand the game better. For example, goal-line technology helps referees determine whether the entire ball has crossed the goal line. I want to learn more about how these technologies work and what new innovations could be introduced in the future.

My backup topics were **Law** and **Government**. Although these topics also interested me, I decided that soccer was the best choice because it is something I already enjoy and would be excited to research throughout the school year.

Some sources I explored include [FIFA's Goal-Line Technology page](https://football-technology.fifa.com/innovation/innovating-the-game/goal-line-technology) and [the IFAB's VAR protocol](https://www.theifab.com/laws/2025-26/video-assistant-referee-var-protocol/). These sources explain how technology is used in soccer and how it helps referees make decisions. They will help me understand my topic before I begin developing my website.

## Engineering Design Process

I am currently in the beginning stages of the Engineering Design Process (EDP). One of the first steps is defining the problem that I want my project to address. For my project, the problem is that people may not know enough about the existing and future technologies that are changing soccer.

The next step is research. I plan to explore different soccer technologies, learn how they work, and investigate their advantages and disadvantages. After gathering enough information, I will be able to organize my research and decide how to present it on my website. My goal is to create a website that informs viewers about soccer technology in a way that is interesting and easy to understand.
